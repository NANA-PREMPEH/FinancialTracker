Core Equity Accounting Standards in Financial Tracker

1. Capital is not Revenue

Injecting personal cash or partner funds increases wallet liquidity and owner equity directly. It never passes through the Income Statement or inflates revenue.

2. Memo Reinvestments

Allocating profit into project funding or reinvestments is an internal allocation. It does not double-count equity or trigger artificial cash transactions.

3. Automated Journal Sync

External movements automatically update the selected cash wallet and post double-entry balancing entries to your Chart of Accounts.

Equity Accounting Guide

Principles & rules behind your capital tracking

The Core Equity Equation

Total Equity = Contributed Capital + Retained Earnings

Contributed Capital = Initial Capital + Additional Injections + Partner Contributions − Owner Drawings

✓
Why reinvestments are memo entries

Reinvesting earnings takes profit already generated by operations and re-allocates it toward a project or capital reserve. Because that profit is already included in Retained Earnings, adding it again would artificially double-count your net worth.

✓
Why capital injections are not revenue

Money you invest in your own company increases your cash and your owner equity balance. It is not customer revenue and therefore is never taxed as business income.

✓
Automated Journal Entries

When an external movement is recorded, Financial Tracker credits/debits the chosen wallet and updates Owner Equity in the Chart of Accounts simultaneously.
